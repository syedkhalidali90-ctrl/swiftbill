SwiftBill POS - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

SwiftBill POS ("the app") is a billing, Khata (customer ledger) and stock app for shops, published by Syed Khalid Ali. This page explains what data the app uses and where it is kept.

1. Your shop data stays on your phone

Bills, customers, vendors, Khata balances, products, stock and settings are stored only in the browser storage of the phone or computer where you use the app. They are not uploaded to our servers.

2. Licence registration

To activate the app, you enter your shop name, owner name, mobile number and city. The app sends these, together with a random device code, the app version and the phone's browser description, to the publisher's licence register (a Google Sheet owned by the publisher). This is used only to manage trials and licences and to contact you about your licence. It is never sold or shared with anyone else.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose "Connect Google Drive", the app asks Google for the drive.file permission. With it, the app can only create and read the backup files that it creates itself in your own Google Drive. It cannot see, change or delete any other file in your Drive. Backups go directly from your phone to your Drive; the publisher never receives them. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

SwiftBill's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. E-invoices and WhatsApp

When you send an e-invoice or an order, the app opens WhatsApp with the message. The invoice details travel inside the link you send; they are not stored on any server.

5. Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes while the scanner is open. No pictures or video are saved or sent.

6. Deleting your data

You can erase all data in the app (Settings → Erase All Data). To delete your licence registration, contact us below.
